# 🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard built on top of a pizza sales dataset, focused on figuring out what's actually driving the business — which pizzas sell, when people order, and where there's room to improve.

---

## 📌 About the Project

I started this by writing SQL queries to explore the raw data — total revenue, best-selling pizzas, order timing, that kind of thing. Once I had a good handle on the numbers, I moved everything into Power BI to build something visual and interactive instead of just staring at query outputs.

Some of the questions I wanted answered:
- How much revenue are we making, and what's the average order worth?
- When do most orders come in — which hours, which days?
- Which pizzas and categories are actually pulling their weight?
- Are there pizzas that barely sell and could be dropped from the menu?
- Do people prefer certain sizes, and does that change by pizza type?
- Are most orders just 1-2 pizzas, or do people order in bulk?

---

## ✨ What's on the Dashboard

Split across three pages, each with a different focus.

### 📊 Sales Overview
The big-picture numbers — total revenue, orders, average order value, plus how revenue trends over time, when the rush hours hit, and how cumulative revenue builds up over the year.

### 🍕 Product Performance
Where the money actually comes from. Top-selling pizzas, top performers by category, the highest-priced pizza compared to the average, and (just as important) the worst-performing pizzas — the ones that might not be worth keeping on the menu.

### 📦 Order & Size Patterns
More behavioral — what size people order most, whether that changes depending on the pizza category, and whether most orders are small (1-2 pizzas) or bigger, which matters if you're thinking about upsell offers.
